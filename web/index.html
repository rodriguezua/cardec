Saving behavior for capital not spent at time zero

The default assumes a disciplined investor. Someone who avoids a large down payment does not automatically invest the difference, so the other two options drop the investment return: "held as cash" keeps the unspent money, "spent or dissolved" does not. All three produce the same vehicle costs; only the opportunity-cost charge and the residual balance change.
